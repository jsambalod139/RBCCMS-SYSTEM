Privacy & Account Information

How your information is used in RBCCMS.

Information collected

RBCCMS stores your account information, assigned office, profile photo, notification choices, and concern updates you record.

Why it is used

Your information supports account access, office assignments, concern handling, reporting, and audit records.

Who can access it

Personnel can access concerns assigned to their office. Only authorized Barangay Office personnel manage resident registration and verification records. Administrators oversee accounts, offices, and reporting.

Notification choices

Your preferences control the notifications displayed in RBCCMS. Turning them off does not remove concern records or status history.

Corrections and account assistance

You can edit your name and login email in Account settings. For office-assignment corrections or other account assistance, contact the system administrator.

Protect your account

Use an email inbox you can access, choose a strong password, and never share your password or reset link. Log out when using a shared device.
